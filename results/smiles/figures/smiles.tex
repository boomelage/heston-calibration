\noindent The smiles above are the Heston model's fitted OTM prices on March 16, 2020.
The fit uses 182 calibration cells across 12 maturities and 30 strikes,
backed by 578,198 contracts of traded volume.
The reference spot was $S_{\mathrm{ref}} = 3254.0554$,
priced under a risk-free rate of 0.92\% and a dividend rate of 1.85\%.
The intraday spot range was 0.96\%.
Fit quality is 0.52 vol points of implied-volatility RMSE,
with a relative-price RMSE of 0.0402.
The Feller condition violates at this calibration,
with $2\kappa\theta - \eta^2 = -1.5935$.
